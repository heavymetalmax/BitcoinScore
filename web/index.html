₿ Bitcoin Buy Risk — Як працює індекс

Версія: квітень 2026 р. · 11 індикаторів · 16 мілстоунів 2018–2026 рр.

Що таке Bitcoin Buy Risk

Bitcoin Buy Risk — інтегральний індекс, що вимірює поточну ринкову позицію Bitcoin за шкалою 0–100:

  • 0–20: капітуляція / екстремальне дно
  • 20–40: дискаунт-зона, накопичення
  • 40–60: нейтральна зона, невизначеність
  • 60–75: дистрибуція / перегрів, обережність
  • 75–100: екстремальний перегрів, зона продажу

Індекс складається з двох рівнів:

  • On-chain score (50%): NUPL, MVRV Z-score, SOPR, Addr in Profit, RHODL Ratio, CVDD Ratio
  • Tech/Macro score (50%): CipherB, SMC, Fear & Greed, DXY, Global M2 YoY, Geopolitical Risk

₿ Bitcoin Buy Risk — How the Index Works

Version: April 2026 · 11 indicators · 16 milestones 2018–2026

What is Bitcoin Buy Risk

Bitcoin Buy Risk is a composite index measuring the current Bitcoin market position on a 0–100 scale:

  • 0–20: capitulation / extreme bottom
  • 20–40: discount zone, accumulation
  • 40–60: neutral zone, uncertainty
  • 60–75: distribution / overheating, caution
  • 75–100: extreme overheating, sell zone

The index consists of two groups:

  • On-chain score (50%): NUPL, MVRV Z-score, Addr in Profit, RHODL Ratio, CVDD Ratio
  • Tech/Macro score (50%): CipherB, SMC, Fear & Greed, DXY, Global M2 YoY

Key Findings

1. The index clearly distinguishes bottoms from tops

Average at confirmed bottoms (4 events): 18.5  |  At confirmed ATHs (4 events): 81.8
A 63-point gap — statistically significant for a binary buy/sell signal.

2. Declining peaks effect

CycleATHBitcoin Buy Risk
2021 spring$63,50091
2021 autumn$69,00086
2024$73,50082
2025$129,00068

Despite a new price ATH in 2025 (+76% vs 2024 ATH), Bitcoin Buy Risk peaked at only 68. The market is maturing: lower score volatility means less speculative overheating. In 2025, the sell zone starts at ~65, not ~80 as in 2021.

3. SMC — most accurate binary indicator

SMC perfectly separates all 4 bottoms (0) from all 4 ATHs (100) with zero false signals. It measures the current price between a confirmed weekly swing low and swing high.

4. Fear & Greed: confirms extremes, but not the 2025 ATH

F&G at the 2025 peak showed only 50 (Neutral) at $129,000. Reason: institutional capital (ETFs, corporations) drives price without mass retail euphoria.

5. Global M2 YoY: liquidity benchmark

2024 ATH had M2 score = 30 — rally driven by Halving + ETF, not monetary expansion. 2021 ATH had M2 = 94 — COVID QE flooded markets with liquidity.

6. On-chain group: most reliable long-term

  • OC at bottoms: 18–31
  • OC at 2021 ATH: 84–91
  • OC at 2024–2025 ATH: 63–77 — MVRV Z-score no longer reaches extremes due to the grown Realized cap

7. Tech/Macro: sensitive to short-term events

  • 2022 capitulation: Tech = 7 (CipherB=0, SMC=0, F&G=6)
  • Pre-bull 2020: Tech = 71 (SMC=100, M2=83)
  • Post-ATH dump 2025: Tech = 32 (SMC=56, CipherB=14)

Methodology Limitations

  • Geopolitical Risk: excluded from backtest, weight 4% — minimal impact on final score.
  • Global M2 gap: BMP data lags ~6 months. US WM2NS delta used as proxy for current values.
  • Map function calibration: MVRV and RHODL recalibrated for current market reality. Comparison with 2013/2017 is not applicable.
  • SMC retroactive: computed retrospectively on weekly OHLCV trimmed to milestone date.
