Meta Score — Confidence-weighted Blend

When V3 and V5B agree → equal weight (50/50).
When they disagree → V5B gets more weight (it's predictive; V3 is diagnostically confused).
conf = 1 − |V3 − V5B| / 100
w_v3 = conf / 2        ← 0 to 0.5
w_v5b = 1 − w_v3      ← 0.5 to 1.0
Meta = w_v3 × V3 + w_v5b × V5B
