Philosophy

There is no single correct way to evaluate the risk of buying Bitcoin. Anyone claiming otherwise is offering certainty where none exists. This index is a mathematical framework designed to minimize emotional biases, news noise, and short-term market psychology.

Rather than relying on speculative chart drawing, this index evaluates whether a given moment is historically cheap or historically expensive based on hard macroeconomic conditions and block-by-block user behavior.

"Be fearful when others are greedy, and greedy when others are fearful."

— Warren Buffett

How the Index Is Constructed

The final index value is a weighted combination of 10 indicators across two distinct categories. Both sub-scores are calculated independently and cross-blended to build the final reading:

  • On-Chain (OC) Sub-score (50% final weight): 80% On-Chain metrics + 20% Tech/Macro metrics.
  • Tech / Macro Sub-score (50% final weight): 80% Tech/Macro metrics + 20% On-Chain metrics.
  • Final Risk Index: 50% OC Sub-score + 50% Tech/Macro Sub-score.

On-Chain Indicators (OC)

NUPL — Net Unrealized Profit/Loss (30% weight)

Measures the difference between total market unrealized profit and unrealized loss divided by Bitcoin's market capitalization. It captures the overall emotional state of holders: values >0.75 represent euphoria, while values <0 show total capitulation.

MVRV Z-Score (20% weight)

Calculates the standard deviation distance between Bitcoin's market capitalization and realized capitalization (the total cost basis of all moved coins). A high score (e.g., >7) means market price is unsustainably detached from holder value, while values below 0 represent deep value.

RHODL Ratio (20% weight)

Compares the amount of coins held for 1 week against those held for 1–2 years. When the ratio surges, it signals that inexperienced new money is rapidly absorbing supply from seasoned holders—a classic cyclical peak behavior.

CVDD Ratio (15% weight)

Cumulative Value Days Destroyed divided by the current price. It maps the aggregate destruction of "coin-days" (holding time multiplied by amount). In cyclical downturns, the price hitting this ratio level has historically marked absolute cycle bottoms.

aSOPR — Adjusted Spent Output Profit Ratio (15% weight)

Measures the average profit multiplier of spent coins (excluding day-trades under 1 hour). To filter noise, we apply a 7-day Simple Moving Average (SMA). If aSOPR drops to 1.0 (break-even point) and bounces, the bull market remains intact. We map aSOPR = 0.88 to 0 risk (maximum fear/capitulation) and 1.12 to 100 risk (local market extension).

Tech / Macro Indicators

Cipher B Oscillator (50% weight of Tech/Macro)

An implementation of the WaveTrend momentum oscillator evaluated on a weekly timeframe. The weekly scale filters out daily noise, pinpointing major momentum shifts: 0 indicates deep correction/capitulation, and 100 represents an overextended bull market.

Mayer Multiple (20% weight of Tech/Macro)

Calculates the simple ratio between Bitcoin's price and its 200-day moving average (200DMA). It acts as a mean-reverting trigger, mapped linearly: values ≤0.5 represent extreme undervaluation, and ≥2.1 represent major overvaluation.

Fear & Greed Index (10% weight of Tech/Macro)

A contrarian sentiment proxy measuring social media, search trends, volatility, and volume. We use a 7-day moving average instead of the daily reading to avoid transient spikes. A lower score (Fear) signals accumulation opportunities; a high score (Greed) alerts to caution.

Yield Curve Spread (10% weight of Tech/Macro)

The spread between US 10-Year and 2-Year Treasury bonds. When the spread goes negative (inversion), it signals systemic liquidity starvation, tightening financial conditions, and high recession risk, which pushes the BTC risk score higher.

Global M2 YoY Growth (10% weight of Tech/Macro)

Tracks the year-over-year rate of change of global money supply (US, EU, China, Japan). High M2 expansion indicates system-wide debasement and liquidity inflows (low risk of asset collapse). Negative M2 growth indicates global monetary contraction (higher risk).

Funding Rate (Display Only)

The 7-day average of Bybit BTCUSDT perpetual futures funding rate. While shown on the dashboard, it is not included in the weighted risk calculation due to its high volatility and reduced signal reliability in the institutional ETF era.

Key Findings

1. High Separation Gap

The index exhibits a significant separation between market cycle bottoms and tops. The average score at confirmed bottoms (2018, COVID, Capitulation, FTX) is 21. The average score at confirmed tops (2021 spring/autumn, 2024, 2025 tops) is 82.75. A ~62-point separation makes this index highly reliable for long-term positioning.

2. Diminishing Peaks Phenomenon

Despite Bitcoin making a new price peak in late 2025 ($129,000, representing +76% over the 2024 high), the risk index only peaked at 70. This demonstrates that as mature, institutional capital (ETFs and corporate balance sheets) drives price, the underling on-chain metrics (such as MVRV Z-score and RHODL waves) do not reach the extreme speculative levels seen in 2018 or 2021. Takeaway: The practical cycle exit window starts at ~70+ in the modern era, rather than the ~85+ seen in earlier cycles.

3. Complimentary Sentiment

While on-chain metrics are the anchor of cycle-scale trends, technical/macro signals are sensitive to shorter-term events. For example, during the Post-ATH Dump in November 2025, the On-Chain score remained structurally neutral (61), but the Tech/Macro score collapsed to 19, signaling a healthy, low-risk tactical buying window inside a structural trend.
